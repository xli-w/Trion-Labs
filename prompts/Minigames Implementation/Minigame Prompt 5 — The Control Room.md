You are refining the fifth and final mini-game in Trion Labs: **The Control Room**.

Work within the existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture and Trion visual system.

## Objective

Create a polished 60–90 second interaction demonstrating Trion's ability to turn operational data into useful decision-making.

This should be the final major challenge before the completion summary.

The visitor should understand that the goal is not to display more data. It is to provide the right information to the right people so they can make better decisions.

## Story

Management wants to know what is happening across the operation.

There is plenty of data, but no clear operational view. Different people need different information, and the existing dashboards are either too detailed, too disconnected, or focused on the wrong things.

The visitor must build a useful operational view.

## Interaction

Present a selection of available information:

- Throughput
- Quality
- Delivery
- Downtime
- Machine status
- Material availability
- Open quality issues
- Production schedule
- Maintenance alerts
- Cost indicators

The visitor should choose which information belongs in a useful operational view.

Then allow them to assign or prioritise the information for different audiences:

- Management
- Production
- Quality
- Planning
- Maintenance

Do not make this a full dashboard builder.

Keep it focused on selecting, prioritising, and connecting the information that matters.

## Decision

The visitor should make choices such as:

- Which KPIs should be visible?
- Which information should be grouped together?
- What should be prioritised?
- Which alerts matter?
- Who needs to see what?

The game should reward clarity rather than simply selecting the most information.

## Feedback

Show the operational view becoming more useful.

For example:

- Important issues become easier to identify.
- Relevant information is grouped together.
- Unnecessary metrics are removed.
- Different roles receive more relevant views.

Use feedback such as:

> “The best dashboard is not the one with the most information. It is the one that helps someone make the right decision.”

## KPI impact

Update:

- Visibility
- Productivity
- Delivery or Throughput, where appropriate

The main improvement should be **better decision-making**, not an arbitrary large KPI increase.

## Upgrade

Unlock:

**Central Operational View**

This should feel like the culmination of the previous integrations.

The visitor should see the connected operation represented in one coherent view.

## Design requirements

- Make the dashboard-building interaction clean and satisfying.
- Use a refined operational control-room aesthetic.
- Avoid creating a generic BI dashboard.
- Make the information hierarchy clear.
- Use subtle animation when the view becomes more coherent.
- Maintain the Trion white / #281a39 visual system.

## Success criteria

The visitor should finish understanding:

> “The right information, presented to the right people, enables better decisions.”

This should feel like the natural final challenge of Trion Labs.
