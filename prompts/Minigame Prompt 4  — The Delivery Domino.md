You are refining the fourth mini-game in Trion Labs: **The Delivery Domino**.

Work within the existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture and Trion visual system.

## Objective

Create a polished 60–90 second dependency and decision-making interaction demonstrating how connected information helps an operation respond to disruption.

The visitor should understand that a problem in one area can affect several others.

## Story

A late material delivery threatens several customer orders.

The issue begins in logistics, but its consequences spread through production, planning, and delivery.

The visitor must trace the dependency chain and choose where to intervene.

## Interaction

Show a simplified connected operational flow:

**Material delivery → Production schedule → Available capacity → Customer orders → Delivery commitments**

Include a small number of affected items.

The visitor should be able to click through the chain and see:

- What is affected.
- What depends on it.
- How much time is available.
- Which orders are at risk.
- Where an intervention could reduce the impact.

Do not make this a complex supply-chain simulator.

The goal is to demonstrate relationships and consequences.

## Decision

Present 2–3 possible interventions.

Examples:

- Improve logistics visibility.
- Connect material and production planning.
- Introduce earlier alerts.
- Improve exception handling.

The visitor should choose the intervention that best addresses the actual problem.

## Feedback

Animate the dependency chain as the visitor investigates.

When an intervention is selected, show the consequences.

For example:

> “The material delay was visible in logistics, but its effect on production and customer orders was not. Connecting the information allows the issue to be acted on earlier.”

Show affected orders becoming less at risk after the improvement.

## KPI impact

Update:

- Delivery
- Visibility
- Throughput or Cost, where appropriate

Avoid unrealistic perfect outcomes.

## Upgrade

Unlock:

**Logistics + Production Visibility**

Show the operational model becoming more connected.

## Design requirements

- Make the dependency chain the visual centrepiece.
- Use clean lines and connected nodes.
- Make the consequences easy to follow.
- Use subtle animation to show information flowing through the operation.
- Keep the interaction simple.
- Maintain the Trion white / #281a39 visual system.

## Success criteria

The visitor should finish understanding:

> “Connected information helps people see problems earlier and act before they spread.”

This game should make the overall operation feel more interconnected than before.
