You are refining the third mini-game in Trion Labs: **The Spreadsheet Shuffle**.

Work within the existing vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture and Trion visual system.

## Objective

Create a polished 60–90 second workflow improvement interaction demonstrating Trion's **Simplify → Standardise → Automate** approach.

The visitor should experience the difference between a fragmented process and a connected, efficient one.

## Story

A planner spends the morning reconciling spreadsheets.

Information is copied between systems, manually checked, and emailed to different people. The work is repetitive, slow, and prone to errors.

The visitor must improve the workflow.

## Interaction

Show a simple workflow with 6–8 steps.

Example:

1. Receive production update.
2. Open spreadsheet.
3. Copy production figures.
4. Check ERP information.
5. Reconcile differences.
6. Email planning update.
7. Wait for confirmation.
8. Update schedule.

The visitor should be able to:

- Identify unnecessary steps.
- Remove duplicate work.
- Reorder steps where appropriate.
- Connect information sources.
- Choose which steps should be automated.

Do not make the interaction frustrating.

Use drag-and-drop only if it is genuinely intuitive. Click-to-select and click-to-remove may be better on mobile.

## Decision

Give the visitor a small improvement budget or limited number of changes.

They should choose which changes create the most value.

Examples:

- Simplify the workflow.
- Standardise the information.
- Automate repetitive updates.
- Connect ERP and planning information.

The choices should have different consequences.

## Feedback

Show the workflow becoming visibly simpler.

For example:

- Fewer steps.
- Fewer handoffs.
- Less duplicated information.
- Faster completion.
- Better consistency.

Show a before/after comparison.

Use a message such as:

> “The biggest improvement was not adding another tool. It was removing unnecessary work and making the information flow consistent.”

## KPI impact

Update:

- Productivity
- Visibility
- Cost

Potentially improve Delivery slightly if planning information becomes more reliable.

## Upgrade

Unlock:

**Workflow Automation**

Explain that automation is now being applied to a clearer, more standardised process.

## Design requirements

- Make the before/after transformation visually satisfying.
- Use a clean process-flow layout.
- Make manual steps feel slightly cumbersome without becoming cartoonish.
- Use subtle animation when steps are removed or connected.
- Keep the interaction focused.
- Maintain the Trion white / #281a39 visual system.

## Success criteria

The visitor should finish understanding:

> “The best automation often starts by simplifying the process.”

This should be one of the most satisfying games in the experience.
